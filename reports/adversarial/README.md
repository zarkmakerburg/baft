# Adversarial resilience & intrusion reports

The periodic resilience tester (`tests/adversary/`) acts as a censor / DoS
attacker and as an on-path intruder against a real BAFT EX/IR pair on loopback,
then **leaves a report** of what it tried and what held. Reports are published
three ways:

- the **job summary** of every `adversary-soak` workflow run (Actions tab);
- an uploaded **artifact** (`report.md` plus the raw `results.jsonl`), 30-day
  retention;
- a persistent, browsable history on the **`adversarial-reports` branch**
  (`latest.md` + `history/<timestamp>.md`), updated on every scheduled run.

The snapshot below is a real run committed as the baseline. It is regenerated by
`tests/adversary/report.py` from a run's results and is loopback only — the
tester never targets any third-party host.
